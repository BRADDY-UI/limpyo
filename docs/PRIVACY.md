# Limpyo Privacy Policy (draft)

*Version 1 · November 2026 · Draft for review. Not legal advice. Limpyo follows the principles of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173): transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality.*

Limpyo is an independent, open-source project. It is **not** an official app of the City Government of General Santos or of any barangay.

## What we collect, and why
| Data | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | To sign you in and send account emails | Only you (and the database itself) |
| Display name | Shown on leaderboards (you can hide yourself) | Other users, if you choose |
| Barangay | To show your collection schedule and trucks | You; staff of your barangay |
| Home pin (optional) | Only for "truck nearby" alerts | Only you. Never shown to anyone |
| Reports: photo, note, location | To fix the problem you reported | You and staff of that barangay |
| Push notification token | To send you the alerts you turned on | Only our server |
| Language, theme, notification settings | To set up the app the way you like | Only you |

**Guests** (no account) can browse schedules, the map, the guide and the scanner. Nothing about guests is stored on our servers. The barangay a guest picks is saved only on their phone.

## What we never do
- Sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Track your location in the background. "Use my location" takes one rough reading, only to pick your barangay. It's worked out on your phone and is not sent or stored.
- Collect your phone number, birthday or contacts.

## Photos
Before a report photo leaves your phone, faces are blurred, text (such as plate numbers) is pixelated, and all hidden photo data (EXIF, GPS) is removed. Photos are stored privately and deleted 60 days after the report is closed.

## Drivers
Truck drivers' locations are shared only during an active shift, and the driver's identity is never shown to residents.

## How long we keep data
| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Report photos | until the report is closed + 60 days |
| Reports (text, location) | 2 years, then the location is rounded to about 100 m |
| Notifications | 90 days |
| Deleted accounts | personal data removed within 24 hours; your reports stay, without your name, for barangay statistics |

## Your rights
You can see and download your data, correct your profile, and delete your account at any time in **Settings**. Agreeing to this policy is required to create an account. Separate choices (sharing scanner photos, appearing on leaderboards) can be changed at any time.

## Security
Every rule about who can see what is enforced by the database itself, not just the app. Staff accounts require two-step verification. To report a security problem, see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## Contact
Open an issue at https://github.com/BRADDY-UI/limpyo, or use private vulnerability reporting for security matters.

*If a city or barangay ever adopts Limpyo officially, it becomes the personal information controller and this policy will be updated.*
